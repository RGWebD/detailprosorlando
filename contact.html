FAQ

Good questions.

Straight answers from what Detail Pros publishes.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Detail Pros is fully mobile. They come to your home or office seven days a week across Orange, Seminole, Lake and Osceola counties.

What can you detail?

Cars, trucks and SUVs, boats, RVs (motorhomes, 5th wheels, travel trailers and mobile homes) and motorcycles, including Harleys, Victory, sport bikes and customs.

How much does a detail cost?

From their current published menu: Premium Interior from $139.95, Signature Detail from $169.95 and Ultimate Detail from $199.95, depending on vehicle size. Premium Wax is $99.95 and add-ons start at $16. Boats, RVs and motorcycles are quoted by phone.

How do I book?

Call or text 407-456-0031, or email detailprosorlandoinc@gmail.com. Appointments are required. There is no online booking system, so a quick call is the fastest way.

What are your hours?

Their Google listing shows 8 AM to 8 PM, seven days a week. Call to confirm a time.

What payment do you accept?

Credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments, per their Google listing.

Do you offer ceramic?

Ceramic wax is available as a $30 add-on, or as a $40 upgrade on the Premium Wax. Ask about IGL ceramic coating options, which are quoted on request.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. The team serves customers in English and Spanish.
